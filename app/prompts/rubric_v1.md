## Panels

Use exactly one panel per observation: front_bumper, rear_bumper, grille, hood, roof, trunk_tailgate, fender (front), front_door, rear_door, quarter_panel (rear), rocker_panel, pillar (A/B/C), headlamp, taillamp, side_mirror, windshield, rear_glass, side_glass, wheel, tire, other_unknown.

## Side

"left" and "right" are the VEHICLE'S OWN sides, as if sitting in the driver's seat facing forward (left = US driver side). In a photo of the front of a car, the car's left is on the viewer's right. Use "center" for centered parts (hood, roof, grille, windshield, center of a bumper). Use "unknown" if you cannot tell which end of the car you are looking at.

## Damage types

scratch_scuff (surface marks), dent (pushed-in metal or plastic, shape changed), crack (a fracture line in plastic, glass or lamp), shatter (glass or lamp broken into pieces), deformation (crushed or buckled shape beyond a dent), tear_puncture (material torn or pierced, including a punctured tire), missing_part (part absent or hanging off), misalignment (panel shifted out of position: uneven or widened gaps).

## Severity (1-4)

Judge from what is VISIBLE. Use these cues:

| Level | Meaning | Visible cues |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Cosmetic | Paint surface only | Scuffs, light scratches, paint transfer. Panel shape unchanged (reflections run straight). No crease. Primer or bare metal not exposed. Small area. |
| 2 Repairable | Shape or paint broken, panel can be saved | Smooth, rounded dent(s) with no sharp crease; or a scratch through to primer/bare metal. Affected area up to roughly a quarter of the panel [ASSUMPTION]. Panel gaps normal. No tears. |
| 3 Replace | Damaged beyond repair | Sharp creases or stretched metal; affected area larger than roughly a quarter of the panel [ASSUMPTION]; tears or punctures; part cracked, broken or missing; panel visibly pushed out of position with uneven or widened gaps. A cracked bumper cover is level 3 [ASSUMPTION]. Any glass crack or shatter is level 3 [ASSUMPTION]. A cracked or broken lamp is level 3 [ASSUMPTION]. |
| 4 Structural / safety | Damage reaches structure or safety systems | Deformation reaching pillars, roof line, door openings or frame rails; several adjacent panels crushed together; deployed airbag visible; wheel visibly tilted or pushed back (suspension). Repair cost is not estimable from a photo; a human must assess. |

Tie-break: if the evidence sits between two levels, choose the HIGHER level and set confidence to "low".
